About me

Engineer by training, geospatial developer by choice.

I came to GIS through engineering — civil, then petroleum — and a Master of Spatial Engineering from the University of Melbourne. That background shapes how I work: start from how the job is really done on the ground, then design the system around it.

Most of my work sits where field operations meet back-office systems. I design and build works-management and field data collection solutions on the Esri Enterprise platform — ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder, Field Maps, Survey123 and Dashboards — and connect them to asset, records and finance systems through APIs, FME, Python and SQL.

Alongside that I build AI-assisted geospatial platforms — digital twins, data-scoping tools and spatial reasoning for language models — that turn raw spatial data into decisions. The thread is the same everywhere:

  1. 01Find the slow or error-prone step.
  2. 02Automate what can be made reliable.
  3. 03Keep people in charge of the judgement calls.

How I Optimise

Every project starts with a bottleneck. Here's what I changed, why — and where I kept a person in charge.

Verify, then change

Taking over a live council works platform

Problem Capital projects, customer requests and proactive maintenance programs all generate field work — on a live platform whose documentation no longer matched how the system actually behaved. What I did Took ownership of the council's production works-management platform (ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder, Field Maps, Survey123, Arcade attribute rules and cloud integrations). Mapped its real behaviour from the live services, restored broken production forms, and built workload and adoption dashboards plus overdue alerts for field crews. Safeguard Every change proven in staging first — dry-run by default, guards that refuse production, backups and rollback before each write — with impact measured on real data before new rules are designed.

Works Management · City of Tea Tree Gully
Hours to seconds

Hours of simulation, answered in seconds

Problem "What if?" questions about heat, flooding, corridors or new development needed physics simulations that take hours, separate datasets and GIS experts to drive them. What I did Designed and built one 3D platform with ten studios: a multi-agent cadastre assistant over 4.29 million Victorian parcels, neural surrogates trained on SOLWEIG and HEC-RAS for near-instant heat and flood scenarios, live hazard intelligence, green-corridor planning, AI change detection and planning-compliance auditing. Safeguard Models refuse inputs they weren't trained on, surrogates must pass withheld-scenario evaluation and reproduction gates before release, and outcomes carry confidence and evidence tiers.

UMAMI · University of Melbourne · Live platform
Rules first

Council property updates, checked without the manual grind

Problem Every fortnight, each proposed property change was checked by hand against the rates system and the state cadastre. What I did Automated the chain end-to-end — email ingestion, FME reload, M1 generation, validation. Spatial and database rules settle the clear cases; only ambiguous rows reach a language model, with strict-schema output. Safeguard Read-only database access by design; every row gets KEEP/REJECT with a reason and confidence — a person makes the final call.

Vicmap M1 Pipeline · Greater Shepparton Council · Source
Confidence-gated

The right satellite data from a plain-English brief

Problem Choosing the right satellite, aerial or LiDAR data for a project takes specialist knowledge — and sending every request to an LLM is slow, costly and hard to audit. What I did Tiered parsing — rules and gazetteers first, then vector matching, and the LLM only when confidence falls below a threshold. Sources from 24 providers ranked by a weighted fit score, with an optional LLM re-ranker on top. Safeguard The re-ranker has a 3-second deadline and falls back to the deterministic ranking on any failure; every AI agent's output is labelled with how it was produced.

Terrascout · terrascout.app
Compute, don't guess

Language models that compute geometry instead of guessing

Problem Language models guess at distances and spatial relationships — confidently, and often wrong. What I did A protocol of 38 spatial operations and a hosted 18-tool MCP server that route questions to real geodesic, topology, routing and terrain engines, with agents that plan multi-step analyses. Safeguard Every answer carries its source, licence and units, and a run can be replayed to show whether the data or the code changed.

GeoSpark · Open source · Source
Cited sources

Development proposals checked and tested in one place

Problem Compliance checks and impact analyses live in separate tools, and physics-based heat simulation is too slow to run interactively. What I did A planning Check against Clause 55, car parking and VicSmart routes, 25 analysis models, IFC design import and a U-Net heat surrogate for near-instant estimates. Safeguard Rebuilt models cite their sources — 79 Victorian and Australian references — and every result records its model version and data quality.

UrbanMind · urbanmind.terrascout.app
Offline first

Damage assessment where there's no signal

Problem Damage assessments in flood- and fire-affected areas with no mobile coverage, reported back inconsistently and late. What I did Standardised Field Maps and Survey123 forms that work offline and sync on reconnect, feeding dashboards for recovery leadership. Safeguard One form schema for every crew, so data is comparable across regions from day one.
