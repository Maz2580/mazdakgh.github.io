About Me

GIS Systems Analyst & Developer

I'm a GIS Systems Analyst & Developer with a Master's in Spatial Engineering from the University of Melbourne. I've spent my career in government GIS — emergency recovery at Parks Victoria, field and asset systems at Mansfield Shire, cadastral automation at Greater Shepparton — and I now develop the works-management solution for the City of Tea Tree Gully on the ArcGIS platform.

In parallel, I'm a Data Engineer at CSDILA (University of Melbourne), and I build geospatial platforms end-to-end: Terrascout and UrbanMind are live, and GeoSpark — an open-source library that lets language models compute spatial answers instead of guessing them — is published on PyPI.

The thread through all of it is optimisation: find the step that is slow, manual or error-prone, automate what can be made deterministic, make every decision traceable, and keep people in control of the judgement calls.

How I Optimise

Every project starts with a bottleneck. Here's what I changed, why — and where I kept a person in charge.

Verify, then change

Taking over a live council works platform

Problem Capital projects, customer requests and proactive maintenance programs all generate field work — on a live platform whose documentation no longer matched how the system actually behaved. What I did Took ownership of the council's production works-management platform (ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder, Field Maps, Survey123, Arcade attribute rules and cloud integrations). Mapped its real behaviour from the live services, restored broken production forms, and built workload and adoption dashboards plus overdue alerts for field crews. Safeguard Every change proven in staging first — dry-run by default, guards that refuse production, backups and rollback before each write — with impact measured on real data before new rules are designed.

Works Management · City of Tea Tree Gully
Hours to seconds

Hours of simulation, answered in seconds

Problem "What if?" questions about heat, flooding, corridors or new development needed physics simulations that take hours, separate datasets and GIS experts to drive them. What I did One 3D platform with plain-language spatial queries, neural surrogates trained on SOLWEIG and HEC-RAS for near-instant heat and flood scenarios, green-corridor planning, AI change detection and a natural-language cadastre. Safeguard Surrogates judged on whole-domain evaluation, outcomes shown with confidence and evidence tiers, and an explainable optimiser instead of a black box.

UMAMI · University of Melbourne · Live platform
Rules first

Council property updates, checked without the manual grind

Problem Every fortnight, each proposed property change was checked by hand against the rates system and the state cadastre. What I did Automated the chain end-to-end — email ingestion, FME reload, M1 generation, validation. Spatial and database rules settle the clear cases; only ambiguous rows reach a language model, with strict-schema output. Safeguard Read-only database access by design; every row gets KEEP/REJECT with a reason and confidence — a person makes the final call.

Vicmap M1 Pipeline · Greater Shepparton Council · Source
Confidence-gated

The right satellite data from a plain-English brief

Problem Choosing the right satellite, aerial or LiDAR data for a project takes specialist knowledge — and sending every request to an LLM is slow, costly and hard to audit. What I did Tiered parsing — rules and gazetteers first, then embeddings, and the LLM only when confidence falls below a threshold. Sensors ranked by a weighted fit score. Safeguard Every AI agent has a deterministic fallback, and its output is labelled with how it was produced.

Terrascout · terrascout.app
Compute, don't guess

Language models that compute geometry instead of guessing

Problem Language models guess at distances and spatial relationships — confidently, and often wrong. What I did A protocol and MCP tools that route spatial questions to real geodesic and topology engines, plus a 535-question benchmark comparing models with and without tools. Safeguard Results reported honestly — including where tools or prompting made a model worse.

GeoSpark · Open source · Source
Fast surrogate

Heat and planning scenarios planners can test live

Problem Planners test "what if" scenarios, but physics-based heat simulation is too slow to run interactively. What I did A U-Net surrogate trained on simulation outputs for near-instant heat estimates, alongside planning models parameterised to Plan Melbourne and Australian standards. Safeguard One model library behind both the QGIS plugin and the web platform, so results never drift between tools.

UrbanMind · urbanmind.terrascout.app
Offline first

Damage assessment where there's no signal

Problem Damage assessments in flood- and fire-affected areas with no mobile coverage, reported back inconsistently and late. What I did Standardised Field Maps and Survey123 forms that work offline and sync on reconnect, feeding dashboards for recovery leadership. Safeguard One form schema for every crew, so data is comparable across regions from day one.
